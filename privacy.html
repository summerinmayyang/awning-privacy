Privacy

The Awning · Last updated 27 October 2026

The Awning holds the things a parent is carrying. That is private by nature, so the app is built so there is nothing to leak.

Nothing leaves your phone

The Awning makes no network connections. There is no server, no account, and nothing to sign up for. Everything you put into the app is stored on your device and nowhere else. We cannot see it, because it never reaches us.

Speech stays on the device

When you speak into the app, your words are transcribed on your phone using Apple's on-device speech recognition. Audio is not recorded, not stored, and not sent anywhere.

If a device cannot transcribe locally, The Awning refuses to transcribe at all and asks you to type instead. It will not send your voice to a server to save you the typing.

The microphone

The microphone is used only while you are actively speaking a note, and only after you have given permission. You can withdraw that permission at any time in iOS Settings, and the app will still work by typing.

Your child's information

If you enter a child's name and date of birth, that information is stored on your device along with everything else. It is not transmitted, not analysed, and not shared.

No tracking

There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking of any kind, and no third-party code in the app that could collect anything.

Deleting the app

Because everything lives on your device and there is no backup on our side, deleting the app deletes everything in it permanently. There is no copy anywhere for us to restore.

During the beta

This version is distributed through Apple's TestFlight. Apple collects its own crash and usage information about TestFlight builds under Apple's privacy policy, not ours. If you send feedback through TestFlight, it reaches us with whatever you choose to include.

Changes

If the app ever starts storing or sending anything beyond your device, this page will say so before that version ships.
